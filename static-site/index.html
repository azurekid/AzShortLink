Short links that keep your audience to yourself.

AzShortLink turns long URLs into short, memorable links azhk.in. Every redirect is a plain HTTP 302 — no interstitial page, no advertising, no third-party scripts and no cross-site tracking cookies.

Request access Sign in

No ads, ever

Visitors are redirected straight to the destination without hitting any landing page, countdown and nothing to skip.

No third-party trackers

No analytics SDKs, advertising pixels or social widgets are loaded. The redirect response contains only the destination.

Analytics you own

Click counts, coarse geo lookup and referrer data stays in your own account and are only visible to you.

Privacy by default

No visitor cookies are set on redirects, identifiers are hashed before stored and a strict Content-Security-Policy is enforced.

QR codes and an API

Generate a QR code for any link and automate everything through a documented REST API with per-account keys.

Passkey authentication

Sign in with a passkey instead of a password. Phishing-resistant, device-bound and backed by WebAuthn.

Hosted in Europe

Your links, accounts and analytics are hosted on Azure infrastructure in the EU.

Reward program

Earn rewards for referring new users to AzShortLink. Details about the reward program will be shared with eligible accounts.

Request an invite

AzShortLink is currently invite-only, which keeps the service free of spam and abusive redirects. Leave your email address and a short note about how you plan to use the service, and an administrator will send you an invite link. Your address is used for that invite only and is never shared or used for marketing.
